# PROP HUNT v2.0.0
PropHunt - Hide as valuables, hunt the fakes. Multiplayer game mode.

![Prop Hunt](https://i.ibb.co/0VdzCTyP/Hi.gif)

## How To Play

* Enemies are disabled by default with PropHunt installed.
* One player becomes the Hunter. Everyone else becomes a Hider.
* Hiders look at any Valuable and press **V** to disguise as it.
* Press **V** again to clear your disguise.
* Press **C** to toggle first-person or third-person disguise view.
* Press **X** to lock your prop in place. You can still look around, but the visible prop stays still.
* The Hunter gets a handgun with infinite ammo.
* If the Hunter shoots a disguised Hider, that Hider dies.
* Every Hunter shot costs the Hunter **5 HP**, so spamming shots can kill the Hunter.
* If all Hiders die, the Hunter wins.
* If the Hunter dies or time runs out, the Hiders win.
* After each round, the Hunter rotates and a random new level loads.

## Round Flow

* **Pre-hide:** Hiders choose props while the Hunter is locked in place.
* **Active round:** Hunter searches with the handgun while Hiders stay hidden.
* **Round end:** Winner banner appears, the next Hunter is chosen, and the next level loads.

## Config

- You can change the main settings in your mod manager config:
* Hide time
* Hunter lock time
* Round length
* Hunter gun range
* Hider disguise key
* Hider view key
* Hider position lock key
* Enemy spawn disabling
* Valuable spawn boosting

## Compatibility

**ALL PLAYERS NEED THE MOD INSTALLED!**
**ONLINE ONLY - YOU NEED OTHERS TO PLAY WITH!**
- Prop Hunt is meant to be played by itself but Custom levels and custom valuables are mostly compatible and are the best kind of extra content for Prop Hunt, unless a specific mod conflicts with how Prop Hunt handles levels or valuables.
- Game-altering mods may or may not be compatible, especially mods that change player movement, weapons, health, rounds, level loading, or core networking. Do NOT ask me to fix compat issues, I will not do it.
